About the project

One connected pipeline, from preferences to itinerary

AI Travel Planner combines a supervised travel dataset, a trained scikit-learn recommendation model and the Gemini API into a single product. You enter a total trip budget in rupees, the number of days, who you are travelling with, the season and the things you actually care about.

The ML layer converts those inputs into the exact feature representation the model was trained on — encoded duration and budget level plus nine interest ratings — scales them with the saved StandardScaler and returns the closest destinations by cosine similarity. The AI layer then plans the trip for the destination you pick.
