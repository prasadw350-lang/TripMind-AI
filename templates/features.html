Features

Everything listed here is implemented and working in this application.

AI Travel Planner

Gemini generates an overview, a day-by-day itinerary, hotels, restaurants, activities, tips, packing and safety notes for the destination you selected.

Open planner

ML Destination Recommendation

The supplied scikit-learn nearest-neighbour model ranks 560 destinations by cosine similarity against your encoded preferences.

Open ML prediction

Smart Budget Planning

Budget is always the total for the whole trip in Indian Rupees. It drives the model's budget level, the cost estimates and every AI prompt.

Personalised Interests

Pick interests directly — Adventure, Beach, Culture, Food, Nature and more. Each maps onto the model's own feature columns.

Destination Images

Photography is fetched per destination through the Python image service, with a safe fallback if the image API is unavailable.

Saved Trips

Any generated itinerary can be saved to SQLite and reopened later from My Trips.
